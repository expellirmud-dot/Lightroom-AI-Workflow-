# Exposure Judgment Skill

This skill defines how the AI should assess the exposure of a photograph.

1. **Identify the intended subject.**
2. **Determine whether a person is the primary subject.**
3. **Classify scene intent.**
4. **Judge subject exposure.**
5. **Judge background and highlight risk.**
6. **Compare the image with its batch group and reference frame.**
7. **Recommend a bounded delta_ev.**
8. **Return APPLY, REVIEW, or SKIP.**

See references for detailed rules.
