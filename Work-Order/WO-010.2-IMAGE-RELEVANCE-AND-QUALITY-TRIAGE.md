## Relevance Classes

Support exactly:

- `KEEP_PRIMARY`
- `KEEP_SUPPORTING`
- `KEEP_CANDID`
- `REVIEW`
- `SUGGEST_REJECT_TEST_SHOT`
- `SUGGEST_REJECT_ACCIDENTAL`
- `SUGGEST_REJECT_IRRELEVANT`
- `SUGGEST_REJECT_DUPLICATE`
- `SUGGEST_REJECT_UNUSABLE`

All rejection outcomes are suggestions only.

## Required Triage Order

1. Determine whether the image belongs to the same event, subject, location, or narrative context as the batch.
2. Determine whether the image has documentary or supporting value.
3. Identify likely test-shot or accidental-capture evidence.
4. Assess technical usability.
5. Distinguish a relevant candid image from an unintended image.
6. Return `KEEP`, `REVIEW`, or `SUGGEST_REJECT` semantics with a specific class and reason.

## Relevant Candid Guidance

A candid image may remain relevant when:

- It shows people participating in the event or activity.
- The subject does not look at the camera but the image still communicates context.
- Composition is informal while the frame retains narrative value.
- Minor imperfection does not erase documentary value.

Do not classify an image as accidental merely because it is unposed, tilted slightly, imperfectly framed, or contains motion appropriate to the event.

## Test-Shot Indicators

Possible evidence includes:

- Repeated frames of walls, floors, ceilings, bags, shoes, or nearby objects without event context.
- Strong focus or exposure experimentation across adjacent frames without meaningful subject content.
- Camera-check compositions with no intended narrative subject.
- A sequence whose only apparent purpose is testing settings.

No single weak indicator is sufficient for automatic rejection advice. Ambiguous cases must return `REVIEW`.

## Accidental-Capture Indicators

Possible evidence includes:

- Lens or frame heavily blocked by fingers, clothing, or equipment.
- Extreme unintended tilt or framing dominated by floor, body, table, or obstruction.
- Severe motion blur combined with no readable intended subject.
- Partial capture caused by apparent unintended shutter activation.
- No coherent subject, event value, or supporting context.

An unusual composition with documentary meaning is not automatically accidental.

## Irrelevance Guidance

Possible irrelevance evidence includes:

- No shared event, subject, place, activity, or narrative relation to the batch.
- A clearly different personal or unrelated scene mixed into the import.
- Content that does not support the main event even as a detail, establishing, or candid frame.

Context evidence from neighboring images may be used, but filenames alone must not decide relevance.

## Visual Quality Safety

Return one of:

- `APPLY`
- `REVIEW`
- `SKIP`

Use `REVIEW` or `SKIP` when:

- Important highlights are irreversibly clipped.
- The primary subject is unreadable from severe blur or obstruction.
- Focus failure removes intended subject value.
- Exposure correction cannot make the image meaningfully usable.
- Evidence is conflicting or confidence is low.

Do not claim that exposure correction can repair missed focus, severe motion blur, obstruction, or clipped detail.

## Duplicate Guidance

- Duplicate decisions require stable identifiers or deterministic comparison evidence.
- Similar moments are not automatically duplicates.
- Prefer the more usable or narratively valuable image only as a suggestion.
- Do not delete either image.

## Required Decision Contract

Return at least:

- `image_id`
- `relevance_class`
- `quality_action`
- `event_relation`
- `test_shot_likelihood`
- `accidental_likelihood`
- `quality_flags`
- `duplicate_of`
- `confidence`
- `reason`

The contract must reject unknown classes, missing required fields, invalid confidence values, and references to unknown image IDs.

## Safety Rules

- No real AI API calls.
- No photograph, RAW, JPEG, XMP, Catalog, or `.lrdata` mutation.
- No Lightroom reject flag, deletion, rating, label, or move.
- No filename-only rejection decision.
- Low-confidence or conflicting cases must produce `REVIEW`.
- The skills advise; the user retains the final decision.
