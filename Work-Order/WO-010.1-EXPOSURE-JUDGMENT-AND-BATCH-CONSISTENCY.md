## Required Judgment Model

The decision order is:

1. Identify the intended subject.
2. Determine whether a person is the primary subject.
3. Classify scene intent.
4. Judge subject exposure.
5. Judge background and highlight risk.
6. Compare the image with its batch group and reference frame.
7. Recommend a bounded `delta_ev`.
8. Return `APPLY`, `REVIEW`, or `SKIP`.

## Definition of Balanced Exposure

Balanced exposure is not simply average frame brightness.

For a person-focused image, exposure is balanced when:

- The primary face and relevant skin retain usable detail.
- Eyes, nose, mouth, and facial structure remain readable.
- Skin is not lifted until texture or highlights clip.
- Bright clothing and important highlights retain detail.
- The intended atmosphere of night, stage, backlight, or indoor light remains believable.
- The person remains the visual priority even when the background average is already balanced.

When the overall frame appears balanced but the primary person is materially dark, prefer a modest positive adjustment subject to highlight safety.

When the person is balanced but the background is dark by scene intent, do not brighten solely to normalize the background.

## Delta EV Guidance

- `±0.10` to `±0.25` EV — minor correction
- `±0.30` to `±0.50` EV — normal correction
- `±0.55` to `±0.75` EV — major correction requiring strong evidence
- Greater than `±0.75` EV — default to `REVIEW`

All recommendations must still obey configured `maximum_delta_ev`.

Do not invent precision unsupported by evidence. Prefer quarter-stop or similarly bounded practical values.

## Batch Consistency Requirements

- Group images by materially similar scene, lighting, location, and subject context.
- Do not force one exposure baseline across different lighting environments.
- Select a representative reference frame per group.
- Preserve legitimate variations caused by composition or scene intent.
- Flag large adjacent exposure jumps for review.
- Do not allow one poor frame to become the group reference.
- Reference selection must be deterministic for identical inputs.

## Required Decision Contract

Return a structured result containing at least:

- `image_id`
- `subject_type`
- `subject_exposure`
- `background_exposure`
- `scene_intent`
- `highlight_risk`
- `group_id`
- `reference_image_id`
- `recommended_delta_ev`
- `action`
- `confidence`
- `reason`

The contract must reject unknown enum values, non-finite numbers, missing required fields, and out-of-contract actions.

## Safety Rules

- No real AI API calls.
- No XMP reads or writes.
- No Lightroom Catalog or `.lrdata` access.
- No photograph mutation.
- No automatic reject/delete behavior.
- No claim that the skill can recover clipped detail.
- Low confidence or conflicting evidence must produce `REVIEW`.
- Batch consistency must never override subject safety or scene intent.

## Tests
